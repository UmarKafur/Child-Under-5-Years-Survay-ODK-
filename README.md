# Child-Under-5-Years-Survay-ODK-
KoboToolbox data collection form for malaria surveillance among children under 5
Child Under 5 Years Malaria Survey (KoboToolbox/ODK Project)
Overview

This project demonstrates the design and development of a digital data collection form using KoboToolbox (ODK) for collecting information on malaria cases among children under five years of age.

The form was created to improve the accuracy, consistency, and efficiency of field data collection while reducing paperwork and manual data entry.

Project Objectives
Collect demographic information of children under five.
Support malaria surveillance and monitoring.
Improve the quality and accuracy of field data collection.
Demonstrate practical skills in KoboToolbox and ODK form development.
